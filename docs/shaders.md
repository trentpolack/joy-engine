# Authored Shaders
Engine GPU programs are standalone WGSL files under `crates/joy-renderer/src/shaders/` and `crates/joy-renderer/src/scene_effects/shaders/`. Rust loads them with `include_str!`; wgpu translates them for WebGPU, WebGL 2, Vulkan, Metal, and DirectX 12.

Shading models own their material bindings and surface response through the [shading-model contract](materials.md#shading-models-and-the-wgsl-contract); the renderer owns vertex layouts, depth/blend state, frame targets, pass order, lighting (light clusters, the G-buffer layout, and deferred lighting passes), shadows, and resource lifetime. Keep reusable scene, lighting, particle, terrain, and display programs in the engine.

A game whose surfaces need a look the built-in models cannot express registers its own shading model, which the engine composes into every scene pass. Looks a shading model cannot express belong in the engine's other facilities (materials, the post-process volume, or camera framing); a project never creates its own backend device or render pipeline. Explain coordinate conventions, packed uniforms, color space, and non-obvious numerical choices in shader comments. Do not generate substantial shader source from formatted strings.
